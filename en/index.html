Features

What the app offers

Driver mode

Fast reporting by the passenger and distance-based warnings while driving. The driver may only operate the app when the vehicle is safely stopped.

Live track status

Yellow sectors highlight local hazards; red status messages indicate a full track closure after serious incidents.

Explore screen

Weather at the Ring, tourist drive calendar, webcams, first-timer information and SOS and emergency features.

Map & filters

Viewing spots, parking, food, fuel stations, Ring locations and other places in the region.

Personalisation

Animations on or off, warning distance set to late, standard or early, and different warning sounds.

Multiple languages

German, English, Dutch, French, Italian and Spanish.

From the community

More eyes at the Ring can make the day safer.

Nürburg Guide relies on its community. Reports can help warn others earlier. Official flags, light signals, track rules and on-site instructions always take priority.
