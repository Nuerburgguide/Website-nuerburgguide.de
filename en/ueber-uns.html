About us

Born from a real connection to the Nordschleife.

Nürburg Guide was created because we were missing a simple, modern and clear app for drivers, spectators and visitors around the Nordschleife.

Our goal is not to replace official systems. It is to make orientation, information and community reports easier to access.

Who we are

We are two friends with a strong interest in motorsport, technology and app development. Nürburg Guide grew from that combination: an independent project for visitors around the Nordschleife who want to find what they need on site more quickly.

The app should make a day at the Ring clearer, not more complicated: important places, weather information, tourist drive details, maps, notices and community reports in one structured interface.

Responsibility

The app is an information service. Official rules, flags, signals and on-site instructions always take priority.

Respect the Region

Consideration matters.

We support the idea behind “Respect the Region”: visitors to the Nordschleife should respect the region, its people and its surroundings through considerate behaviour, clean spaces, responsible driving and respectful interaction.
